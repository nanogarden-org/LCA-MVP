## Executive synthesis

LCA has evolved through three layers:

1. **Origins:** preserve a person’s mission, values, heuristics, stories, decision patterns, and hoped-for future continuity.
2. **Revision:** reject the idea that a convincing persona, codex, or model is equivalent to the person; preserve terrain, provenance, context, contradiction, development, authority, and rights instead.
3. **MVP:** implement an inspectable, reversible, governed continuity system that can preserve sources, build multiple maps, generate bounded responses, record every transformation, accept living-source correction, and classify Portrait/Bud/Branch transitions without claiming consciousness transfer.

## MVP boundary

The first MVP is an **Archive + Pensieve + Bamboo-Grove + Provenance Ledger + Invariant/Authority Layer + Review Loop**.

It can demonstrate:

- durable source preservation;
- typed episodes, claims, interpretations, decisions, weather, invariants, transformations, and responses;
- append-before-overwrite versioning;
- provenance and authorship separation;
- lexical, temporal, and graph retrieval;
- contradiction and supersession preservation;
- privacy and authority enforcement;
- source review and correction;
- model portability and recovery.

It cannot demonstrate:

- subjective consciousness transfer;
- a complete export of autobiographical or phenomenological human experience;
- that a Portrait or Bud is literally the living source;
- that P/C/F/A values are measurements of identity rather than governance heuristics.
