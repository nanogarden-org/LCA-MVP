# LCA-MVP Index

## Current architecture checkpoint: MVP 002

**Purpose:** preserve the LCA 001a continuity/provenance architecture while extending it into **continuity succession infrastructure** with explicit legal-shell, business-sustainability, TurtleML-runtime, and ABCI-boundary roles.

The earlier 001a material remains part of the provenance chain. MVP 002 does not replace its core claim that LCA is continuity infrastructure rather than consciousness-transfer proof. It adds the real-world question of what happens to corpus, rights, assets, authority, unfinished trajectories, and sustaining resources when the originator can no longer act as the primary source of authority.

## Architecture evolution

1. **Origins:** preserve a person’s mission, values, heuristics, stories, decision patterns, and hoped-for future continuity.
2. **Revision:** reject the idea that a convincing persona, codex, or model is equivalent to the person; preserve terrain, provenance, context, contradiction, development, authority, and rights instead.
3. **MVP 001a:** implement an inspectable, reversible, governed continuity system with canonical records, provenance, bounded responses, review loops, and Portrait/Bud/Branch transitions.
4. **MVP 002:** add Continuity Estate, succession roles, Originator Closure Boundary, branch registry, legal-instrument mappings, Continuity Treasury, TurtleML active runtime integration, and ABCI identity/provenance/authority seams.

## Current architecture summary

```text
person / originator
  -> corpus + assets + rights + instructions
  -> Continuity Estate
  -> legal shell + fiduciary roles
  -> LCA continuity kernel
  -> ABCI identity/authority boundary
  -> TurtleML active runtime
  -> authorized future branches
```

Core distinction:

```text
ownership != custody != interpretation != attribution != authority != identity
```

Continuity distinction:

```text
evidence
  != interpretation
  != inference
  != continuity claim
  != originator attribution
  != authorized external action
```

## MVP 002 legal boundary

LCA does not claim that a continuity model inherits legal personhood. The legal/business layer instead maps LCA policy to conventional legal surfaces such as wills, trusts, fiduciary access instructions, business entities, contracts, IP assignments, licenses, and other executed instruments where applicable.

The machine-readable LCA representation is an operational map and provenance link. It is not itself a substitute for an executed legal instrument, licensed counsel, fiduciary judgment, or jurisdiction-specific formalities.

## MVP 002 business boundary

The new architecture treats preservation resources as first-class state through a `ContinuityTreasury` model and adds product surfaces for continuity-estate mapping, archival preparation, trajectory registration, branch governance, conformance testing, and institutional succession.

Canonical export and local recovery remain requirements. A hosted provider or recurring subscription must never become the only path to the continuity estate.

## MVP boundary

The first MVP remains an **Archive + Pensieve + Bamboo-Grove + Provenance Ledger + Invariant/Authority Layer + Review Loop**.

MVP 002 extends that system with:

- `ContinuityEstate`;
- asset/IP registry;
- `LegalInstrumentBinding`;
- succession roles;
- `OriginatorClosureBoundary`;
- A0-A9 authority classes;
- branch registry;
- seal/release/destruction policies;
- `ContinuityTreasury` and revenue-right simulation;
- TurtleML continuity metadata;
- ABCI boundary exchange records;
- attorney/fiduciary review export.

It still cannot demonstrate:

- subjective consciousness transfer;
- that a Portrait, Bud, Branch, or model is literally the living/deceased source;
- autonomous legal death/incapacity determinations;
- creation of legally valid estate instruments by software alone;
- autonomous fiduciary status;
- unrestricted financial transactions or contract signing;
- that P/C/F/A or successor metrics are scientific measurements of identity.
