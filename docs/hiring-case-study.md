# LCA-MVP — engineering case study

## Problem

Knowledge systems can blur original evidence, generated interpretation, custody, and authority as people, models, or operators change.

## What I built

I designed a continuity and succession architecture with a runnable 001a foundation and shared Python/Rust conformance fixtures.

## Constraints

Preserve source distinctions across replacement of systems and custodians, while keeping software policy separate from externally granted authority.

## Decision

Separate ownership, custody, interpretation, attribution, authority, and identity; use shared fixtures to compare narrow Python/Rust semantics.

## Working result and verification

001a executable foundation and narrow conformance boundary; 002 succession, estate, treasury, and integration features are architecture and implementation plans.

[Python reference](../reference_impl/python/README.md) · [Conformance commands and scope](../conformance/README.md)

Recorded MVP 001a checkpoint: 11/11 Python behavior tests passed, both language runners passed 18/18 fixtures, and all 18 cross-language comparisons matched.

## Limits

Conformance verifies selected decisions and hashes, not the entire platform or signer identity. MVP 002 originator-closure and succession features remain planned. Software mappings do not create legal authority.

## Applications

Knowledge governance, continuity planning, portable records, policy boundaries, and cross-language verification.
