# LCA-MVP — engineering case study

## Problem

Knowledge systems can blur original evidence, generated interpretation, custody, and authority as people, models, or operators change.

## Contribution

Designed a continuity and succession architecture with a runnable 001a foundation and shared Python/Rust conformance fixtures. This is an author-owned portfolio account of the work; repository history and attribution records provide the inspectable contribution trail. It does not imply independent external validation.

## Constraints

Preserve source distinctions across replacement of systems and custodians, while keeping software policy separate from externally granted authority.

## Decision

Separate ownership, custody, interpretation, attribution, authority, and identity; use shared fixtures to compare narrow Python/Rust semantics.
