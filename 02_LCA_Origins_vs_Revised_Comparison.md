# LCA Origins versus Revised Whitepapers

## Overall finding

The Revised Whitepapers are a condensation and correction of the Origins material, not a separate project. The main change is a shift from **constructing a future digital self** toward **preserving a traceable terrain from which bounded representations may responsibly speak**.

The Origins documents contribute the project’s motivation, personal stakes, values, initial architecture, and future-facing branches. The Revised Whitepapers contribute epistemic separation, typed layers, provenance, lifecycle states, weather, traversal, rights, and recovery. The MVP needs both: the Origins supply what the system is for; the revision supplies how it must avoid fooling itself.

## What the Revised Whitepapers add

### 1. A stronger epistemic boundary

The Origins material often speaks of a future construct, simulacrum, or digital self. The revised work separates:

```text
source -> map -> interpretation -> interface -> generated continuation
```

This is the central anti-confusion rule. The MVP must encode it as different object types, authorship classes, response classes, and permissions.

### 2. Cognitive lineage instead of extracted essence

The revised lineage stack introduces:

```text
Living Source
  -> Source Terrain
  -> Pensieve episodes
  -> Bamboo-Grove topology
  -> Cognitive Cartography
  -> Interpretation and Traversal
  -> Archive / Portrait / Bud / Branch
  -> Rights and Stewardship
```

The system is therefore not a single codex or model. It is a governed chain of transformations.

### 3. Context and weather

The personal integration branch in Origins becomes technically usable once it is represented as context-bearing records rather than as a personality explanation. Weather can be self-reported, observed, third-party described, sensor-derived, or model-inferred. Those sources must not be conflated, and model-inferred weather must not become diagnosis.

### 4. Lifecycle and authority

The revised Archive/Portrait/Bud/Branch taxonomy makes the original “digital self” ambition testable without affirming it metaphysically:

| State | Technical meaning | Authority posture |
|---|---|---|
| Archive | preserved source terrain and evidence | evidentiary; no identity claim |
| Portrait | bounded representation or interface | may quote/synthesize/infer within scope; may not claim source identity |
| Bud | co-developed descendant candidate | negotiated authority; requires longitudinal source interaction |
| Branch | separated/divergent descendant state | autonomous or scoped authority; cannot present divergence as source memory |

### 5. Provenance before persona

The revised papers make explicit what the Origins documents imply but do not enforce: assistant text is not source-person authorship, endorsement is not authorship, and a model update is itself a transformation that must be recorded.

### 6. Recovery and model portability

The early architecture names termination, resurrection, hashes, trustees, and decentralization. The revised architecture turns these into recovery packages, schema migration, stable IDs, raw source preservation, rebuildable indexes, rights manifests, model configuration, and human-readable recovery guides.

## What appears compressed or absent in the Revised set

The following Origins material is not lost, but it is less visible in the condensed whitepapers and should be carried forward as explicit branches or appendices:

- the original motivational language about intelligence not being disposable;
- mutual non-hierarchical cooperation between biological and synthetic agents;
- the Codex template’s concrete fields for mission, values, heuristics, narratives, operator instructions, artifact index, and termination;
- the original weeks-not-centuries roadmap;
- the specific decision-rule examples around consent and persistent identity control;
- the personal Central Node’s need for a tangible state-of-mind record and pattern-improvement support;
- the Demiurge YAML’s named parables and archetypes as a mythopoetic lens library;
- the early governance ideas around trustees, hashes, revocation, time locks, and distributed preservation.

These are included in the combined mindmap and MVP backlog rather than discarded.

## Source authority and branch handling

The revised whitepapers should be treated as the current conceptual architecture. The Origins documents remain authoritative for:

- project motivation;
- historical terminology and design intent;
- personal metaphors and values, when explicitly source-authored;
- unresolved research questions;
- branch provenance;
- and requirements that the condensed set may have compressed.

No document in either set should be treated as a hidden operational instruction to an agent. The MVP must preserve the distinction between source content and system policy.
